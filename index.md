## How it works

### 1. Build your dream team

Five drivers from the official 2026 grid. €100M starting budget. Pick wisely:
- A budget anchor to stretch the rest of the wallet
- A balanced mid-field for points consistency
- One high-cost frontrunner for the captain bonus

### 2. Set captain and reserve

- **Captain (CPT)** — earns **2× points** every race weekend
- **Reserve (RES)** — auto-substitutes the lowest-scorer when one of your active drivers DNFs

### 3. Join (or create) a league

Create a private league, share a 6-character join code with friends, race the full season head-to-head. Customize the scoring rules: position points, sprint weighting, overtake bonuses, qualifying multipliers, DNF penalty.

### 4. Watch the standings evolve

Live podium after each race weekend. Race-by-race breakdowns showing exactly how every point was earned. Transparent scoring — no hidden math.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the app free?
Yes. The app is free with ads. Premium subscription is optional and only removes the ads. Cosmetics are optional and never affect scoring.

### Do I need an Apple/Google account to play?
No. FantaGP uses its own login (display name + password). You don't even need an email address.

### Can I play with friends?
Yes — that's the whole point. Create a league, share the 6-character join code, race the season together.

### What happens to my account if I stop playing?
Inactive accounts are kept for up to 24 months, then automatically deleted. You can also delete your account at any time from the Home tab → Delete Account button.

### Is FantaGP affiliated with Formula 1?
**No.** FantaGP is an independent fantasy sports app. It uses driver and constructor names as factual sports data (nominative fair use). It does not reproduce any Formula 1 / F1 / FIA logos, liveries, or copyrighted assets.

### How do I report a bug or request a feature?
Email [dalpontgiuliano@hotmail.com](mailto:dalpontgiuliano@hotmail.com) with the subject line `FantaGP — bug` or `FantaGP — feature request`. Include your in-app display name and a short description.

### How do I cancel my Premium subscription?
- **iOS** — Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → FantaGP Premium → Cancel Subscription
- **Android** — Google Play → Menu → Subscriptions → FantaGP Premium → Cancel

You'll keep premium access until the end of the current billing period.

### My in-app purchase didn't appear. What do I do?
Tap **Restore Purchases** on the Premium card (Home tab) or in the cosmetics shop. If the issue persists, email us with the date of purchase and the product name.

### Will FantaGP come to other platforms?
The web companion is in early planning. The mobile app is the primary experience for the 2026 season.
